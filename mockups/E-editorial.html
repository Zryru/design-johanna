Good to know

Questions people ask first

How long is a session?

Fifty minutes for individual work, sixty for couples. We can talk about longer sessions if that suits the work better.

Virtual or in person?

Both. I see clients at the Manhattan office and across New York State by video. Plenty of people mix the two depending on the week.

How often would we meet?

Weekly to start. It builds momentum, and momentum is most of the work. We can space things out once you're steady.

What's your cancellation policy?

Twenty-four hours' notice and there's no charge. Life happens — tell me and we'll sort it out.

Do you prescribe medication?

No, that's outside an LCSW's scope. If medication seems worth exploring I'll refer you to a psychiatrist I trust and we'll keep working together.
